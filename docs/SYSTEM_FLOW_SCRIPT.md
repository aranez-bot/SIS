# Student Inquiry System Flow Script

## Opening

Good day everyone. Today, I will present the flow of our Student Inquiry System.

This system is designed to help students submit inquiries online, allow department heads to respond and manage those inquiries, and give the superadmin full control over users, departments, reports, and system settings.

The system has three main user roles:

1. Student
2. Department Head
3. Superadmin

Each role has its own dashboard and set of features.

---

## 1. Student Flow

First, let us start with the student side.

### Step 1: Student Login

The student opens the system and signs in using their student account.

After a successful login, the student is redirected to the Student Dashboard.

### Step 2: Student Dashboard

On the dashboard, the student can see an overview of their inquiries.

The dashboard shows:

- Total inquiries
- Pending inquiries
- In-progress inquiries
- Resolved inquiries
- Recent inquiry activity

This helps the student quickly monitor the status of their requests.

### Step 3: Submit a New Inquiry

To create a new inquiry, the student clicks the New Inquiry option.

The student fills out the inquiry form by selecting:

- The department they want to contact
- The inquiry subject
- The inquiry description

After filling out the form, the student submits the inquiry.

Once submitted, the system saves the inquiry and notifies the assigned department head.

### Step 4: Track Inquiry Status

After submitting an inquiry, the student can go to My Inquiries or Inquiry History.

Here, the student can view all submitted inquiries and check their current status.

The possible statuses include:

- Pending
- In Progress
- Answered
- Resolved
- Closed
- Rejected

### Step 5: View Department Response

When the department head replies, the student can open the inquiry details page.

Inside the inquiry conversation, the student can read the department response and send follow-up messages if needed.

### Step 6: Notifications

The student can also check the Activity or Notifications page.

This page shows updates such as:

- Inquiry submitted successfully
- Department response received
- Inquiry status updated

### Step 7: Download Mobile APK

The student also has access to the Download APK page.

From there, the student can download the Android APK installer and install the mobile version of the system on their phone.

---

## 2. Department Head Flow

Next, let us move to the Department Head side.

### Step 1: Department Head Login

The department head logs in using their department account.

After login, the department head is redirected to the Department Dashboard.

### Step 2: Department Dashboard

The department dashboard shows an overview of inquiries assigned to that department.

It displays:

- Total department inquiries
- Pending inquiries
- In-progress inquiries
- Resolved inquiries
- Recent assigned inquiries

This gives the department head a quick view of their workload.

### Step 3: Manage Inquiry Inbox

The department head opens the Manage Inquiries or Inquiry Inbox page.

Only inquiries assigned to their department are shown.

This means each department head can only manage inquiries related to their own department.

### Step 4: View Inquiry Details

The department head selects an inquiry from the list.

On the inquiry details page, they can see:

- Student information
- Inquiry subject
- Inquiry description
- Current status
- Message conversation

This allows the department head to understand the student concern before responding.

### Step 5: Respond to the Student

The department head can type and send a response to the student.

The response becomes part of the inquiry conversation.

Once a response is sent, the student can view it from their own account.

### Step 6: Update Inquiry Status

The department head can update the inquiry status depending on the progress.

For example:

- Set to In Progress when the inquiry is being handled
- Set to Answered after replying
- Set to Resolved when the concern is completed
- Set to Rejected if the inquiry is not valid

### Step 7: Forward Inquiry

If the inquiry belongs to a different department, the department head can forward it to the correct department.

This helps avoid delays and ensures the inquiry is handled by the proper office.

### Step 8: Department Reports and FAQs

The department head can also view reports and manage department FAQs.

The FAQ section allows them to add common questions and answers for students.

---

## 3. Superadmin Flow

Lastly, let us discuss the Superadmin flow.

### Step 1: Superadmin Login

The superadmin logs in using the system administrator account.

After login, the superadmin is redirected to the Superadmin Dashboard.

### Step 2: Superadmin Dashboard

The superadmin dashboard gives a complete overview of the whole system.

It shows:

- Total students
- Total department heads
- Total departments
- Total inquiries
- Recent inquiries
- System activity

This helps the superadmin monitor the entire inquiry system.

### Step 3: Manage Users

The superadmin can open the User and Admin Accounts section.

Here, the superadmin can:

- Create student accounts
- Create department head accounts
- Edit user details
- Activate or deactivate users
- Delete users if allowed

This gives the superadmin control over who can access the system.

### Step 4: Manage Departments

The superadmin can also manage departments.

In the Departments section, the superadmin can:

- Add new departments
- Edit department information
- Update department email
- Activate or deactivate departments
- Delete departments if allowed

Departments are important because student inquiries are assigned based on the selected department.

### Step 5: Monitor All Inquiries

The superadmin can view all inquiries across every department.

Unlike department heads, the superadmin is not limited to one department.

This allows the superadmin to monitor the full activity of the system.

### Step 6: Reports and Analytics

The superadmin can open the Reports and Analytics page.

This page helps track:

- Inquiry volume
- Department performance
- Status distribution
- Student activity

These reports help the school understand how well inquiries are being handled.

### Step 7: Roles and Permissions

The superadmin can review role-based access.

This ensures that:

- Students can only manage their own inquiries
- Department heads can only manage their department inquiries
- Superadmin can manage the whole system

### Step 8: Audit Logs and Maintenance

The superadmin can also access audit logs and maintenance tools.

Audit logs help track important system actions.

Maintenance tools help with system backup and general administration.

---

## Complete System Flow Summary

The complete flow of the system is:

1. The student logs in.
2. The student submits an inquiry to a selected department.
3. The system saves the inquiry and notifies the department head.
4. The department head views the inquiry.
5. The department head responds and updates the inquiry status.
6. The student receives the update and can reply if needed.
7. The inquiry continues until it is resolved or closed.
8. The superadmin monitors all users, departments, inquiries, and reports.

---

## Closing

In conclusion, the Student Inquiry System provides a centralized and organized way to manage student concerns.

Students can easily submit and track inquiries.

Department heads can respond and manage department requests efficiently.

The superadmin can control the whole system, monitor activity, and manage users and departments.

This makes the inquiry process faster, more transparent, and easier to manage for both students and school staff.
